ReCast for Chrome: privacy

In short: ReCast for Chrome saves pages to the ReCast app on your Mac. Nothing is sent to us. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

What it uses

  • When you save a page: the page’s address, title, words and pictures.
  • On pages you visit: the address of the page’s feed (RSS), if it has one, so it can offer you Follow.

It does not read your passwords, cookies or browsing history.

Where it goes

  • Only to the ReCast app on the same Mac.
  • ReCast keeps what you save in your Library and syncs it to your other devices through your own iCloud account. We can’t see it.
  • Feed addresses from pages you only visit are not kept.

What gets downloaded

To save a page’s pictures or a PDF, or to follow a feed, Chrome and ReCast download them from the site they belong to, just as if you had opened them yourself. For a podcast’s page, ReCast asks Apple Podcasts or Pocket Casts for the podcast’s feed.

Selling and sharing

We don’t sell your data, share it with anyone, or use it for anything except saving pages and following feeds for you.

Removing your data

Delete anything you saved in ReCast and it is removed from your Library. ReCast for Chrome keeps nothing itself.
